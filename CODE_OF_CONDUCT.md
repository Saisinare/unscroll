# Code of Conduct

Unscroll should be a welcoming project for everyone, whatever their background, identity or experience level. This applies to issues, pull requests, discussions and anywhere else you represent the project.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful and assume good intent.
- Keep criticism focused on the work, not the person.
- Be patient with newcomers and with people who are still learning.
- Be kind about screen-time struggles. People may come to this project because of real difficulties with their phone use.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, threats, insults or personal attacks
- Discriminatory language or imagery
- Sexual content or unwanted sexual attention
- Publishing someone's private information without their permission
- Spam, or repeatedly disrupting conversations

## Reporting

- **Issue or comment in this repo:** use GitHub's **Report content** option in the comment's **⋯** menu.
- **Serious abuse or a threat:** [report it to GitHub](https://github.com/contact/report-abuse), which handles it directly.

## Enforcement

The maintainer may edit or remove content, and may warn or block anyone who breaks this code. The response depends on how serious the behaviour is and whether it has happened before.
